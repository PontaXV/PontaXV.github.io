Privacy Policy - LLM mail reader

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Who uses this app

LLM mail reader is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads messages in the owner's Gmail account (read-only access, gmail.readonly).

How the data is used

The messages are processed on computers that the owner runs at home, to create a daily summary for the owner. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Limited Use

LLM mail reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account > Third-party apps & services.
